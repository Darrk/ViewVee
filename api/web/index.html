Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to get started.

Do I need two phones?

Yes. One phone works as the camera and another as the monitor. An older spare phone is perfect for the camera.

Can I watch from outside my home Wi-Fi?

Yes. ViewVee connects directly when both phones share a Wi-Fi network and uses a secure internet relay when they are on different networks.

Is my video private?

Devices pair using a private QR secret. Signaling is authenticated and your media travels over encrypted WebRTC.

Can I pair an iPhone with an Android phone?

Yes. ViewVee supports iOS and Android, including pairing devices across the two platforms.

Where are recordings saved?

Recordings are saved locally on the device that captured them, so you stay in control of your recorded moments.
