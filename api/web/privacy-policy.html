3. End-to-end encryption and server roles

Live video and audio are end-to-end encrypted between paired devices. WebRTC encrypts media in transit, including when a TURN server has to relay the encrypted packets. The Signal server coordinates the connection, and the TURN server forwards packets; neither service is intended to decrypt or store the live media content.

Signaling is sent over secure WSS/TLS and signaling messages are authenticated between paired devices to detect alteration and replay. Connection servers still process the technical data needed to establish and maintain a session, such as session identifiers, roles, IP addresses, connection candidates, and transient signaling messages. Encryption cannot prevent a person using either paired device from viewing, hearing, locally recording, or sharing content available on that device. Keep pairing links and QR codes confidential.

5. Local network use and device permissions

ViewVee requests camera and microphone access for monitoring and two-way communication; local network and Wi-Fi access to find and connect devices on the same network; and notifications to show motion, sound, or connection alerts. On supported devices, foreground-service and wake-lock permissions help an active monitoring session continue when the screen is off. ViewVee does not request precise GPS location. Network information such as IP addresses can still indicate an approximate location.

  • Account and support data is retained while needed to provide the Service, resolve requests, secure the Service, and meet legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized.
